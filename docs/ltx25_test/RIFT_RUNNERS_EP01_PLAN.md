# Rift Runners — The Chrono-Core Heist (6-Character, Space-Vehicles, Lyria 3.5 Hip-Hop & Gemini 3.8 Voices)

## Expanded 6-Character Crew, Voices & Anti-Gravity Space Vehicles

- **Kael (Voice: `Puck`):** Kael, an agile tween-proportioned cobalt-blue and slate-white lynx-fox scout with pointed tufted ears, sharp amber-gold eyes, expressive dark eyebrows, a weathered copper-brown leather vest, fingerless climbing gloves, and brass aviator goggles on his forehead
- **Nyx (Voice: `Kore`):** Nyx, a sleek charcoal-black and silver-grey marten gadgeteer with violet-tipped ear tufts, sharp emerald-green eyes, a tan canvas crossbody satchel, and a glowing cyan crystal wrist-grappler gauntlet on her left forearm
- **Bram (Voice: `Charon`):** Bram, a broad-shouldered stocky rust-red and cream-striped badger-bear heavy with thick groomed fur, expressive bushy eyebrows, warm hazel eyes, a riveted bronze shoulder pauldron, and a coiled climbing rope across his chest
- **Zara (Voice: `Zephyr` — NEW):** Zara, a high-energy sunburst-gold and warm-cream fennec-fox ace pilot with tall expressive black-tipped ears, a luminous turquoise holographic flight visor, a white-and-tangerine cropped flight jacket with brass buckles, and padded pilot gloves
- **Jinx (Voice: `Fenrir` — NEW):** Jinx, a nimble amethyst-violet and pearl-white ring-tailed lemur mechanic and hoverboarder with a long striped purple-and-white tail, bright golden eyes, oversized bronze magnifying goggles, and a teal tool-bandolier
- **Pip (Harmonic Relic Chimes):** Pip, a fist-sized living clockwork-crystal relic shaped like a floating glowing amber-gold sunstone octahedron inside two spinning brass astrolabe rings, with two expressive luminous cyan shutter-eyes

### Space Vehicles & Mobility Fleet

- **Star-Skiff (`Aether-Runner`):** the Star-Skiff (Aether-Runner), a sleek open-cockpit multi-crew anti-gravity space speeder built from brushed brass, cobalt-blue hull plates, and weathered steel, featuring twin side-mounted ion-thruster pods, glowing cyan repulsor rings underneath, a front pilot yoke, and a rear deck rail
- **Mag-Board:** the Mag-Board, a compact brass-and-teal anti-gravity space hoverboard with glowing turquoise repulsor pads and twin neon-cyan plasma micro-jets at the tail
