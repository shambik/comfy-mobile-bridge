The user requested additional characters in blue, pink, purple, white and grey shades. These are new cast members; retain Pip, Momo and Tiki. ## New characters

| Numi | Pearl white, ivory highlights, cool silver shadows | Small upright cloud-soft oval, one forward-falling crown tuft | Dreamy and curious; soft breathy coos, tiny gasps |
| Pebble | Charcoal/soft-black and graphite grey, silver cheeks, mist-grey belly | Squat rounded-square pillow, broad windswept crest | Steady with gentle comic surprise; warm low puffs and chuckles |

Names are working production names. All use groomed 3D fur, expressive eyes, short mitten paws and soft broad feet. Exactly two arms and two feet. No clothing, long ears, horns, wings or tails. Keep silhouettes distinct rather than merely recoloring an existing character. White fur needs silver shadow detail; grey fur needs a readable lighter face. Never turn either into a flat white or black silhouette.
