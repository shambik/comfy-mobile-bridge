# Fluffies — The Little Lost Star

## Story and creative direction

Built in the spirit of *Sunny Bunnies*: one clear problem drives the whole episode from setup to attempts, an emotional low point, a spark of inspiration, teamwork and a heartfelt payoff. Each scene has its own emotion (wonder, startle, shy tenderness, concern, physical comedy, held-breath hope, quiet sadness, inspiration, focused teamwork, farewell, magic, joy and peaceful calm). Laughter is reserved for two earned moments (`S12` sheepish chuckle and `S22` celebration hug) rather than every shot.

- **Selected opener:** vault `1002475080.mp4` (`abaee350db914237bbd563fd49afde89`), 10 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, original audio preserved.
- **Cast:** Blue **Bobo** and pink **Lulu** (from the opener) lead; joined by tall purple **Zuzu**, charcoal/silver **Pebble**, pearl-white **Numi**, and **Twinkle** (a tiny plum-sized glowing five-pointed star).
- **Assembly policy:** Every source scene is kept whole (`--full-scenes`, zero lead-in or tail trims, 1× speed) with native nonverbal sound plus an original synthesized instrumental score (`lost_star_original_score.wav`) ducked underneath.
