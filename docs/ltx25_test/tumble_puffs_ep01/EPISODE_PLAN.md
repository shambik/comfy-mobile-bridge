# Tumble Puffs — Episode 01: The Runaway Berry

A complete 120-second, 20-shot original pantomime episode, using the established Pip, Momo and Tiki designs.

## Story

Three friends prepare a picnic around one oversized blueberry. Each solitary solution sends it further away. Pip acts too quickly, Momo is gently overconfident, and Tiki tries to impose order. A shared leaf hammock finally catches the berry. They return it to the picnic, only to discover an entire berry patch nearby. A tiny butterfly gets the final reaction.

## Music and edit

An original 120 BPM cartoon score uses mallet/plucked motifs for Pip, a warm round low-register motif for Momo, and precise short woodwind-like notes for Tiki. The arrangement develops through curiosity (0–28), bounce/misunderstanding (28–59), a light chase (59–89), teamwork (89–107), and reveal/cadence (107–120). Reaction beats receive brief musical space. Soft woodblocks, boings, leaf swishes and a tiny final flutter are synthesized; no voice samples are used. Music continues across picture cuts so the sequence feels like one episode.
