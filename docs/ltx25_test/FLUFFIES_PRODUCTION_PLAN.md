## New episode: The Great Shortcut

A picnic trip becomes an overconfident shortcut: stepping stones, a splash, a hedge squeeze and a playground slide. The trio learn to move together, set their picnic, then realize the long adventure has brought them back beside their cottage. The journey and friendships carry the comedy; the hamper is simply luggage.

The finished episode is exactly 120 seconds: the eight-second series intro plus 112 seconds of story, all at 24 fps. Every picture transition is a hard cut. Keep generated vocal reactions and effects, with an original instrumental score underneath.
