## Story

Lulu distracts Bobo while the other six friends prepare a surprise party. Momo and Pebble deliver a wobbling cake; Pip and Tiki struggle with a ribbon; Zuzu and Numi decorate the terrace. Their plans become messy, but Bobo makes an icing nose to match embarrassed Pebble and turns the mistakes into the fun. The ending celebrates the friends rather than a perfect cake. The new blue/pink pair leads; all eight characters have purposeful action and comic reactions.
